A Vision For African Gold

A goldsmith who saw heritage as something to be worn

Late Mr. Boma CotterellSmith founded Jeweltec Goldsmiths & Jewelers Ltd with a simple conviction: that African gold traditions deserved the same precision, patience, and reverence as any luxury house in the world and that the surest way to keep a culture alive was to let people wear it.

What began as a single workshop grew, through decades of careful, hand-by-hand craftsmanship, into the Jeweltec Port Harcourt knows today. Every technique practised in our workshop from traditional Woko regalia to fine coin-work chains — traces back to standards he set and taught personally to the goldsmiths who trained under him.

"Gold does not simply decorate a person — it carries where they come from. My work has only ever been to make sure that story is told well."

— Late Mr. Boma Cotterell, Founder

His Contribution

A legacy carried in every piece we make

Preserving Tradition

He researched and documented traditional regalia techniques that might otherwise have faded, ensuring they could be taught and reproduced faithfully for generations to come.

Training Master Goldsmiths

Every goldsmith at Jeweltec today either trained directly under him or was mentored by someone who did — his standards live on in every pair of hands in our workshop.

Building A Trusted House

He built Jeweltec's reputation one relationship at a time, insisting that trust — not volume — was the true measure of a jeweler's success.

Moving Forward, Together

Continuing what he started

The name Sankofa — "go back and get it" — guides how we think about our founder's legacy: honouring where our craft came from as we carry it forward into new pieces, new clients, and new generations. Every Jeweltec creation is, in its own way, a continuation of his work.
