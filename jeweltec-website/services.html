How It Works

Bringing a piece in for service

Most cleaning, repair, and restoration work can be assessed the same day you visit our showroom. For custom commissions, we'll walk through your design, materials, and timeline before any work begins.

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or visit the showroom with your piece or design idea.
  2. Our goldsmiths assess the work and confirm cost and timeline.
  3. You approve the quote, and work begins in our on-site workshop.
  4. Collect your piece, restored or newly made, at the agreed date.
