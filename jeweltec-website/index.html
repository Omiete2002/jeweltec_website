Who We Are

A Nigerian goldsmithing house, built on heritage

We create handcrafted traditional African jewelry — premium gold and silver pieces, cultural accessories, and the restoration work that keeps family heirlooms alive.

  • Every piece shaped, polished and finished by hand
  • One commission at a time — never mass-produced
  • Techniques carried forward from our founder's workshop
